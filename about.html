About NZ Salary Calculator

Welcome to NZ Salary Calculator, New Zealand's premier tool for instant, accurate, and private payroll forecasting.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: To provide Kiwis with the most transparent and user-friendly financial tools to help them understand their take-home pay. We believe that financial transparency shouldn't be complicated or buried in government spreadsheets.

Why Use Our Tool?

Unlike outdated calculators, we provide a real-time, visual breakdown of your PAYE tax, ACC levies, and KiwiSaver contributions. We update our tax engine every year to reflect the latest legislation from Inland Revenue (IRD), ensuring that you have the most current data for the 2025–2026 tax year.

Privacy Record

We take your privacy seriously. Our tools are designed so that your financial information never leaves your device. No data is stored on our servers, making this one of the safest platforms for salary planning in New Zealand.
