Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

At NZ Salary Calculator, accessible from your-domain.co.nz, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by NZ Salary Calculator and how we use it.

1. Data Collection

Our calculator is built to be "Client-Side First." This means that the salary and tax figures you input into our tool are processed directly in your web browser. We do not store, save, or transmit your financial data to our servers.

2. Log Files

NZ Salary Calculator follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. All hosting companies do this and a part of hosting services' analytics. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, NZ Salary Calculator uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

4. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is one of a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

5. Our Advertising Partners

Some of advertisers on our site may use cookies and web beacons. Our advertising partners include: Google AdSense.

6. Consent
